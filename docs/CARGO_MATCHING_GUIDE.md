# Cargo Matching Guide: How to Choose the Right Truck

## 🚛 Understanding Truck Types for Your Cargo

### **For Vehicle Transport (Mombasa to Kampala Example)**

**Best Truck Types:**
- **Car Carrier**: Specialized for vehicle transport with multiple levels
- **Flatbed with Side Rails**: For single vehicles or construction equipment
- **Lowbed**: For heavy construction vehicles or machinery
- **Step Deck**: For vehicles that need extra height clearance

**Required Equipment:**
- ✅ **Straps & Chains**: Secure vehicles during transport
- ✅ **Side Rails**: Prevent vehicles from sliding off
- ✅ **Winch**: Load/unload vehicles safely
- ✅ **GPS Tracking**: Monitor vehicle location
- ✅ **Insurance**: Comprehensive coverage for vehicle damage

**Example Search:**
```
Cargo: 5 Toyota Land Cruisers
Route: Mombasa → Kampala
Requirements: Car carrier, GPS tracking, insurance, experienced driver
```

### **For Different Cargo Types**

#### **1. General Cargo (Boxes, Pallets, Machinery)**
- **Truck Type**: Box Truck, Dry Van, Flatbed
- **Equipment**: Straps, Tarps, Side Rails
- **Special Features**: Loading ramps, lift gates

#### **2. Refrigerated/Frozen Goods**
- **Truck Type**: Refrigerated, Reefer
- **Equipment**: Temperature monitoring, humidity control
- **Special Features**: Frozen (-18°C), Chilled (2-8°C), Ambient (15-25°C)

#### **3. Liquid Cargo (Fuel, Chemicals, Food Grade)**
- **Truck Type**: Tanker
- **Equipment**: Pressure monitoring, leak detection
- **Special Features**: Food grade, chemical resistant, explosion proof

#### **4. Bulk Cargo (Grain, Sand, Construction Materials)**
- **Truck Type**: Bulk, Dump Truck
- **Equipment**: Sealed containers, moisture protection
- **Special Features**: Pneumatic unloading, contamination monitoring

#### **5. Oversized/Heavy Equipment**
- **Truck Type**: Heavy Haul, Lowbed, Step Deck
- **Equipment**: Multiple axles, hydraulic systems
- **Special Features**: Permits, escort vehicles, route planning

#### **6. Dangerous Goods/Hazmat**
- **Truck Type**: Specialized Hazmat
- **Equipment**: Safety systems, emergency shutdown
- **Special Features**: Explosion proof, fire suppression, monitoring

#### **7. Container Transport**
- **Truck Type**: Container Chassis, Power Only
- **Equipment**: Twist locks, corner castings
- **Special Features**: ISO standard compliance, securement systems

## 🔍 Key Questions for Cargo Owners

### **Before Booking a Truck, Ask:**

1. **What type of cargo are you transporting?**
   - General goods, vehicles, liquids, refrigerated, hazardous, etc.

2. **What are the dimensions and weight?**
   - Length, width, height, total weight
   - Does it require special permits?

3. **What are the environmental requirements?**
   - Temperature control needed?
   - Humidity control?
   - Protection from weather?

4. **What are the security requirements?**
   - GPS tracking?
   - Real-time monitoring?
   - Insurance coverage?

5. **What are the loading/unloading requirements?**
   - Forklift access?
   - Loading ramps?
   - Special equipment needed?

## 🎯 Smart Matching Algorithm

### **Priority 1: Cargo Type Match**
- Vehicle transport → Car Carrier
- Refrigerated goods → Reefer Truck
- Liquid cargo → Tanker
- General cargo → Box Truck/Flatbed

### **Priority 2: Equipment Requirements**
- Straps, chains, tarps for securing
- GPS tracking for monitoring
- Temperature control for perishables
- Safety systems for hazardous materials

### **Priority 3: Capacity & Dimensions**
- Weight capacity (tons)
- Volume capacity (cubic meters)
- Length, width, height limits
- Loading/unloading capabilities

### **Priority 4: Route & Permits**
- Border crossing requirements
- Special permits needed
- Route restrictions
- Escort requirements

### **Priority 5: Technology & Monitoring**
- Real-time tracking
- Temperature monitoring
- Shock/vibration monitoring
- Door opening alerts

## 💡 Pro Tips for Cargo Owners

### **1. Always Specify Cargo Type**
- Don't just say "general cargo"
- Be specific: "vehicles", "refrigerated food", "hazardous chemicals"

### **2. Provide Accurate Dimensions**
- Length, width, height in meters
- Weight in tons
- Special handling requirements

### **3. Consider Environmental Factors**
- Temperature requirements
- Humidity control
- Weather protection

### **4. Plan for Loading/Unloading**
- Equipment needed
- Time requirements
- Site access limitations

### **5. Budget for Insurance**
- Cargo insurance
- Liability coverage
- Specialized coverage for valuable items

### **6. Track Your Shipment**
- Real-time GPS tracking
- Temperature monitoring (if applicable)
- Delivery confirmations
